Return & Refund Policy

Lekhpal Pro sells digital subscription plans. This page explains when a payment can be refunded and how to ask for a refund.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short

  • Plans start as soon as you pay, so they are usually not refundable once active.
  • We refund duplicate charges, payments that never activated a plan, and verified faults on our side.
  • Ask within 7 days of payment at billing@lekhpalpro.app.
  • Approved refunds reach your original payment method in 5–7 working days.

01Nature of the service

Our plans give you access to digital automation tools inside the app. There are no physical goods, so there is nothing to return. Access starts as soon as your payment is confirmed, which is why activated plans are generally not refundable.

02When we give a refund

You can get a refund if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same plan (duplicate payment).
  • Money was debited but your plan was never activated, and our team could not activate it.
  • A fault on our side, confirmed by our team, stopped you from using the paid features for a long period.

03What is not refundable

  • Changing your mind after a plan has been activated and used.
  • Unused days on a plan that is still active.
  • Problems caused by government portals being slow, changed or offline, because we do not own or control them.
  • Accounts closed for sharing credentials or breaking our Terms & Conditions.
  • Days left on your plan when you delete your account.
  • Free or promotional days that we gave you.

04Money debited but plan not active

Please do not pay again. Most delayed payments settle on their own within a few minutes.

  1. Wait up to 30 minutes.
  2. Open the Account tab and pull down to refresh.
  3. If the plan still does not show, sign out and sign back in.
  4. Still not active? Email us with your payment reference. If we cannot activate your plan, you get a full refund.

05How to ask for a refund

Email billing@lekhpalpro.app within 7 days of the payment, and include:

  • The email address of your Lekhpal Pro (Google) account.
  • The payment reference or UPI transaction ID.
  • The date and amount of the payment.
  • A short note on why you are asking for a refund.
Email a refund request

06Processing time

We usually reply within 24 working hours. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method and normally arrive within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

07Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. You will not be charged again unless you choose to buy another plan.

08Contact us

Billing and refunds: billing@lekhpalpro.app
Anything else: support@lekhpalpro.app

Support hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
