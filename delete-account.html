Account

Delete your account

Permanently delete your Lekhpal Pro account and the data linked to it. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us to do it for you.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Any days left on your plan are lost and are not refunded. If you sign in again later, you will start with a new, empty account.

01Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lekhpal Pro and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the list of what will be deleted, type DELETE in the box, and tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. Your saved portal passwords are also wiped from that phone.

02Ask us to delete it

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, fill in our account deletion form. Enter the same Gmail address you use for Lekhpal Pro so we can confirm the account is yours.

Open the deletion request form

We delete the account within 7 working days of receiving the form and email you when it is done.

03What gets deleted

From our servers, permanently:

  • Profile and sign-in Your name, email address, profile photo and Google sign-in record.
  • Subscription Your current plan and any days left on it, plus your past plans.
  • Payment history Our records of the payments you made in the app.
  • Portal login ID link The portal ID linked to your account. The ID becomes free to use with a different Lekhpal Pro account.
  • Notification tokens The tokens we use to send notifications to your phone.

On your phone

Deleting in the app wipes your saved portal passwords and account data from that phone. To also remove cached portal pages, or if you used the deletion form, uninstall the app or go to Settings → Apps → Lekhpal Pro → Storage → Clear data.

04What may be kept

  • Payment gateway records: our payment gateway keeps its own transaction records for as long as banking and tax laws require.
  • Analytics: usage data collected by Google Firebase Analytics is not linked to your name or email, and Google deletes it automatically under its own retention settings.
  • Your request: if you contacted support or sent the deletion form, we may keep that request for up to 90 days to finish handling it.

For more about how we handle data, read our Privacy Policy.
