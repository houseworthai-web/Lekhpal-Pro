Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use Lekhpal Pro, why we collect it, and how you stay in control of it.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We store your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your government portal passwords are encrypted and never leave your phone.
  • Your portal login ID is sent to our server so that one ID can only be used by one Lekhpal Pro account.
  • We never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

01Who we are

Lekhpal Pro is an Android app made by Lekhpal Pro India Private Limited, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India ("Lekhpal Pro", "we", "our" or "us"). This policy applies to the Lekhpal Pro app and to this website.

Lekhpal Pro is a private, independent tool. It is not a government app and is not run by any government department.

02Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Each item below says where it is kept.

  • Google account details Your name, email address and profile photo link, which Google shares with us when you sign in. We never see or store your Google password. Stored on our servers
  • Government portal login ID and role The user ID you enter in the Work tab (for example, your Lekhpal ID) and whether you work as a Lekhpal or a Revenue Inspector. It is linked to your account so that the same portal ID cannot be used by a second Lekhpal Pro account. Stored on our servers
  • Government portal passwords Encrypted with AES-256 using your phone's Android Keystore and saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us. On your phone only
  • Subscription and payment records The plan you bought, the amount, start and end dates, and the payment reference from our payment gateway. Your card, UPI and bank details go directly to the payment gateway and never reach our servers. Stored on our servers
  • Notification token A Firebase Cloud Messaging token and your app version, so we can send you announcements and service updates. Stored on our servers
  • Usage and diagnostic data Collected by Google Firebase Analytics: which screens you open, app version, phone model, Android version, approximate region (from your IP address) and an app instance ID. Processed by Google
  • Advertising data (free users) If you do not have an active plan, Google AdMob may use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Subscribers do not see ads. Processed by Google
  • Cached portal pages So the app still works on slow 2G/3G networks, pages and images from government portals are cached on your phone. On your phone only

What we do not collect: your location, contacts, photos, files, SMS or call logs, or hardware identifiers such as your IMEI.

The app asks for only three permissions: internet access, network status and notifications.

03How we use your information

  • To sign you in and show your name and photo in the app.
  • To check your subscription and unlock paid features.
  • To make sure each government portal ID is used by only one Lekhpal Pro account, which prevents shared subscriptions.
  • To process payments, handle refunds and answer billing questions.
  • To send you announcements, update alerts and important service notices.
  • To show ads to users on the free plan.
  • To understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve features.
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

04Government portals and automation

The Work tab opens official government portals inside the app. It can fill in login forms and repeated fields for you using the details you saved.

Whatever you view or submit on those portals, such as land records, case details or forms, travels directly between your phone and the government server. We do not receive or store it.

The automation scripts are downloaded from our servers but run only on your phone. The government portals are run by their own departments and follow their own privacy policies.

05Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of the app for us, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in system.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging and AdMob.
  • Our payment gateway: processes your payments and refunds.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or of Lekhpal Pro, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In the case of a sale, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

06Storage and security

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Our database uses row-level security, so each account can read only its own records.
  • Portal passwords are encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware.
  • Our servers may be located outside India. Wherever they are, we protect your data as described in this policy.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.

07How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, subscription records, payment records, portal ID link and notification tokens are deleted from our database immediately.

Analytics data held by Google is kept for a limited period under Google's own retention settings. Our payment gateway keeps transaction records for as long as banking and tax laws require.

See Delete your account for the full details.

08Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable laws, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you. Just email us to ask.
  • Correct your name from the Account tab, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete your account and data at any time. See Delete your account.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's Android settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (details below). If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

09Children

Lekhpal Pro is made for revenue officials and other adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and we will tell you in the app about any important change.
